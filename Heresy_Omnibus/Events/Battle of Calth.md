# Battle of Calth

The Word Bearers’ surprise assault on [[Calth]], as depicted in [[Know No Fear]], launched under cover of a joint muster with the Ultramarines. It begins with sabotage, orbital catastrophe, and legionary treachery, then collapses into a prolonged fight for survival across the planet and beneath its surface.
