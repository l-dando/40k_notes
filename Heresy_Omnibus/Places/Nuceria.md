# Nuceria

Angron’s home world, where he was enslaved as a gladiator and where his rebellion was broken before the [[Emperor]] took him away. In [[Betrayer]], Nuceria becomes the site of Angron’s return, the destruction of the world’s ruling order, and the battle in which he ascends into daemonhood.
