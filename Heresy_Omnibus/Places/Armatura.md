# Armatura

A war world of Ultramar assaulted during the Shadow Crusade. In [[Betrayer]], the fighting on and above Armatura shows both the World Eaters’ terrifying strength and the collapse of their discipline, while also forcing the *Conqueror* and its commanders into desperate void combat.
