## 🧠 Summary
An Ultramarines captain whose ground-level leadership becomes vital once [[Calth]] descends into darkness, confusion, and attritional warfare.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[Know No Fear]]: Organises scattered resistance on [[Calth]], holding together Astartes, auxilia, and civilians in the aftermath of the Word Bearers’ surprise attack.

## 🔗 Connections
- Serves [[Roboute Guilliman]] and fights beside figures such as [[Aeonid Thiel]] and [[Orfeo Culzean]].
- One of the most important Ultramarines defenders during the [[Battle of Calth]].
