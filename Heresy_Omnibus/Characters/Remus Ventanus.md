## 🧠 Summary
A disciplined Ultramarines captain placed in charge of the Erud Province muster on [[Calth]]. When the attack begins, Ventanus becomes one of the most important organisers among the scattered loyalist survivors on the planet.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Oversees the Erud muster and notices irregular pressure building through the system before open war begins.
- Witnesses the devastation triggered by the orbital betrayal and quickly recognises that it is deliberate.
- Tries to restore communication and order while fighting through shattered muster zones and Word Bearers ambushes.
- Helps turn scattered Ultramarines and survivors into a coherent resistance amid the opening chaos of the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 🔗 Connections
- Commands under [[Roboute Guilliman]].
- Shares Calth’s ground war with figures such as [[Aeonid Thiel]] and other scattered Ultramarines.
- Closely tied to [[Calth]] as one of the principal surface commanders in the novel.

## 📝 Notes
- Ventanus represents Ultramarine steadiness at ground level while high command reels in orbit.
- His sections show how quickly the battle becomes a logistical and civic collapse as well as a military one.
