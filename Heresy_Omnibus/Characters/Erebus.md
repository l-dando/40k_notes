## 🧠 Summary
Erebus is one of the most poisonous figures in the Heresy so far: patient, plausible, and utterly committed to steering other people’s fates. He does not simply serve Chaos. He engineers outcomes, corrodes trust, and treats belief, friendship, and grief as things to be used.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[Horus Rising]]: Steals the Interex anathame and helps set Horus’s corruption in motion.
- [[The First Heretic]]: Helps lead Lorgar toward the Primordial Truth and shapes the Word Bearers’ descent into ritualised heresy.
- [[Know No Fear]]: Stands behind the wider treachery at [[Calth]] as the Word Bearers finally turn grievance into open war.
- [[Betrayer]]: Warns Argel Tal in riddles, resurrects [[Cyrene Valantion]] for his own purposes, and murders [[Argel Tal]] because that death serves the future he wants for [[Khârn]]. He then narrowly escapes Khârn’s vengeance.

## 🔗 Connections
- Chief operative and spiritual corrupter under [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]].
- Instrumental in the falls of [[Horus Lupercal|Horus]] and the Word Bearers alike.
- Bitter enemy of [[Khârn]] after the murder of [[Argel Tal]].
- Repeatedly entwined with [[Cyrene Valantion]] and other figures whose faith or grief he can exploit.

## 📝 Notes
- Erebus is dangerous less because of raw force than because he is willing to betray absolutely anyone.
- In *Betrayer*, he betrays the traitor side from within just as readily as he once betrayed the Imperium.
