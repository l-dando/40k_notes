## 🧠 Summary
A manipulative and calculating Chaplain of the Word Bearers, Erebus is one of the clearest architects of the Heresy in the books read so far. He operates through theft, ritual, corruption, and false diplomacy, always working a step behind the disasters he helps create.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[Horus Rising]]: Travels with the 63rd Expedition under diplomatic pretence and steals the [[Anathame|anathame]] from the Interex, setting later corruption in motion.
- [[The First Heretic]]: Helps guide the Word Bearers’ fall alongside [[Kor Phaeron]], pushing [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]] toward the Primordial Truth.
- [[Know No Fear]]: Appears as one of the hidden hands behind the betrayal at [[Calth]], teleporting onto the planet to prepare the ritual and sabotage that accompany the Word Bearers’ attack.

## 🔗 Connections
- Serves [[Lorgar Aurelian]] and works closely with [[Kor Phaeron]].
- Manipulates [[Horus Lupercal]] and helps steer the wider Heresy toward open war.
- His work links the theft of the [[Anathame]] to later corruption and mass betrayal.

## 📝 Notes
- Erebus rarely needs to lead from the front; his danger lies in preparation, deceit, and timing.
- By Calth, his methods have scaled from personal corruption to legion‑wide massacre.
