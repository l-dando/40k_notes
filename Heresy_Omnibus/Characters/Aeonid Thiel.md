## 🧠 Summary
An Ultramarines sergeant marked for censure because he studies how Space Marines might fight one another. At [[Calth]], that unorthodox thinking becomes one of the XIII Legion’s most useful assets.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Waits aboard the *Macragge’s Honour* for formal rebuke when the Word Bearers attack.
- Arms himself from [[Roboute Guilliman]]’s collection during the daemonic assault on the flagship.
- Saves [[Marius Gage]] and helps stabilise the command response aboard ship.
- Explains that the Ultramarines must abandon predictability and treat fellow Astartes as a practical enemy.

## 🔗 Connections
- Serves under [[Roboute Guilliman]] and proves invaluable to [[Marius Gage]].
- His once‑forbidden theoreticals become central to survival in the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 📝 Notes
- Thiel is one of the clearest examples of Ultramarine doctrine being forced to adapt under pressure.
- His red mark shifts from disgrace to proof that he saw the impossible before anyone else.
