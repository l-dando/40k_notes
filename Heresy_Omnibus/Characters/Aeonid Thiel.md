## 🧠 Summary
An Ultramarines sergeant marked for censure after running simulations about Astartes-against-Astartes combat. When the betrayal at [[Calth]] begins, that supposedly improper imagination makes him one of the Legion’s most effective survivors.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[Know No Fear]]: Moves from red-marked embarrassment to essential battlefield leader, helping civilians and Astartes adapt to the impossible reality of the Word Bearers’ ambush.

## 🔗 Connections
- Serves under [[Roboute Guilliman]] in the Ultramarines.
- Fights alongside other defenders of [[Calth]] such as [[Marius Gage]] and [[Remus Ventanus]].

## 📝 Notes
- Thiel’s “theoretical/practical” method captures one of the novel’s central themes: doctrine matters, but survival demands adaptation.
