## 🧠 Summary
Damon Prytanis is a Cabal agent who enters the story late in *Betrayer*. His appearance matters less for battlefield power than for what it reveals: even at the edge of Angron’s apotheosis, other hidden factions are still trying to shape events from the margins.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Betrayer]]
- Approaches [[Cyrene Valantion]] aboard the *Fidelitas Lex* and attempts to draw her into the Cabal’s purposes.
- Is apparently destroyed when Cyrene’s protector turns on him, only to reappear later during the collapse around Nuceria.
- Becomes part of the final, unsettling shift in Cyrene’s story away from the Word Bearers alone and toward older, stranger designs.

## 🔗 Connections
- Operates for the Cabal, the same hidden faction encountered in [[Legion]].
- Intervenes directly in [[Cyrene Valantion]]’s fate.

## 📝 Notes
- Prytanis’s brief role broadens *Betrayer* beyond Legion warfare and shows that the Heresy is being watched and manipulated from multiple directions.
