## 🧠 Summary
Macer Varren is a World Eaters captain who stands out because he retains more judgement and self-command than many of his Legion brothers. In *Betrayer*, he helps show that the XII Legion’s collapse is not yet total, even if it is accelerating.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Betrayer]]
- Appears among the senior World Eaters navigating the Legion’s fraying discipline during the Shadow Crusade.
- Serves as a contrast to officers who embrace mindless slaughter more completely.

## 🔗 Connections
- Fights under [[Angron]].
- Part of the same command circle as [[Khârn]], [[Lotara Sarrin]], and [[Delvarus]].
