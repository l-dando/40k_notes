## 🧠 Summary
Lhorke is an ancient World Eaters Dreadnought and one of the clearest reminders that the Legion was once something other than what Angron made it. His presence in *Betrayer* ties the XII back to its older War Hounds identity and gives the novel a rare voice of memory inside the Legion.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Betrayer]]
- Is awakened during the battle of Armatura to help defend the *Conqueror* when the ship is left vulnerable.
- Stands with other old veterans and damaged survivors who still preserve fragments of the Legion’s earlier discipline.
- During Angron’s transformation on Nuceria, turns on [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]] rather than accept any further corruption of the Legion.
- Is torn apart when Angron intervenes, showing how completely the old War Hounds have lost the future.

## 🔗 Connections
- Ancient son of [[Angron]], but one whose memory reaches back beyond the Legion’s final moral collapse.
- Acts in uneasy parallel with [[Khârn]] and [[Lotara Sarrin]] as another figure trying, in very different ways, to hold onto something of the XII.
- Opposes [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]] at the decisive moment of Angron’s ascension.

## 📝 Notes
- Lhorke’s last stand matters because it is one of the book’s clearest refusals of what the Legion is becoming.
