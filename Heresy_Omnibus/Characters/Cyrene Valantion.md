## 🧠 Summary
Cyrene Valantion is a human confessor bound closely to the Word Bearers’ spiritual life. Blinded at [[Monarchia]], she becomes both witness and symbol: a mortal figure whose suffering is turned into faith by the Legion around her.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[The First Heretic]]: Survives the destruction of [[Monarchia]], loses her sight, and is elevated by [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]] as a confessor trusted by the Legion and especially by [[Argel Tal]].
- [[Betrayer]]: Though dead at the book’s opening, she remains central to Argel Tal’s grief. [[Erebus]] restores her to life and her sight, but that apparent miracle becomes part of another manipulation. During the fighting around Nuceria and the *Fidelitas Lex*, she is drawn into the schemes of [[Damon Prytanis]] and the Cabal.

## 🔗 Connections
- Deeply important to [[Argel Tal]], who treats her as one of the few people left worth mourning.
- Elevated by [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]] and surrounded by the Word Bearers’ devotional culture.
- Used repeatedly by [[Erebus]], whose gifts are never free.

## 📝 Notes
- Cyrene shows how human suffering becomes sacred currency around the Word Bearers.
- Her return in *Betrayer* is not a restoration of innocence, only a new stage of entanglement.
