## 🧠 Summary
An Ultramarines captain whose trust in the conjunction with the Word Bearers helps show how complete the deception at [[Calth]] really is. Luciel’s role gives the betrayal a personal face.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Greets [[Sorot Tchure]] in good faith during the lead‑up to the muster.
- Stands as a symbol of the possibility that relations between the XIII and XVII might be mended.
- Is killed in the Word Bearers’ treachery when the [[Battle of Calth]] begins.

## 🔗 Connections
- His association with [[Sorot Tchure]] shows how friendship itself is exploited by the traitors.
- One of the Ultramarines officers used to frame the emotional cost of the betrayal at [[Calth]].

## 📝 Notes
- Luciel matters less as a strategist than as proof that the ambush is built on carefully maintained trust.
