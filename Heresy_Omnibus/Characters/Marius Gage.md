## 🧠 Summary
A senior Ultramarines commander at the centre of the legion’s response to the betrayal at [[Calth]]. Gage is one of the first figures aboard the *Macragge’s Honour* to understand how valuable unconventional thinking will be once the Word Bearers reveal themselves.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Helps coordinate the Ultramarines’ response as the muster in orbit collapses into treachery.
- Survives the daemonic assault on the flagship thanks in part to [[Aeonid Thiel]].
- Recognises the practical value of Thiel’s once‑censured ideas and puts them to use immediately.
- Continues to anchor command around [[Roboute Guilliman]] during the opening of the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 🔗 Connections
- Senior subordinate to [[Roboute Guilliman]].
- Forms an immediate and important battlefield partnership with [[Aeonid Thiel]].
- Central to the Ultramarines’ void response at [[Calth]].

## 📝 Notes
- Gage’s quick acceptance of Thiel’s logic shows the Ultramarines adapting at speed once reality becomes impossible to ignore.
