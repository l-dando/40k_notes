## 🧠 Summary
Primarch of the Word Bearers, Lorgar’s insatiable quest for meaning puts him at odds with the [[Emperor]]’s secular vision. His fall from grace ignites the ideological flames of heresy, and his long resentment of [[Roboute Guilliman]] hardens into deliberate revenge.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[The First Heretic]]: Endures censure for his religious fervour, embarks on a pilgrimage into the Warp, and returns as the Word Bearers’ spiritual leader.
- [[Know No Fear]]: His legion’s trap at [[Calth]] turns old bitterness over [[Monarchia]] into open war with the Ultramarines, even if much of the action unfolds through the commanders acting in his name.

## 🔗 Connections
- Mentor to [[Kor Phaeron]] and [[Erebus]].
- His defiance defines the Word Bearers’ path and influences [[Horus Lupercal|Horus]]’s destiny.
- His feud with [[Roboute Guilliman]] shapes the betrayal at [[Calth]].

## 📝 Notes
- Represents faith untempered by doctrine.
- The revenge begun at Calth is ideological as much as military: Monarchia answered through massacre.
