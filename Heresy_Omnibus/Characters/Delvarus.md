## 🧠 Summary
Delvarus is a World Eaters centurion who commands the Triarii, the Legion’s elite boarding specialists. He is formidable, proud, and violent even by XII Legion standards, and his conduct in *Betrayer* shows how badly discipline inside the World Eaters has decayed.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Betrayer]]
- Commands the Triarii contingent assigned to protect the *Conqueror* in void war.
- Chooses unauthorised planetfall at Armatura, leaving the flagship more exposed to Ultramarines boarders.
- Is punished and confined by [[Lotara Sarrin]], showing that even among the World Eaters some derelictions are too dangerous to ignore.
- Remains a feared presence in the Legion’s fighting-pit culture, where brotherhood and brutality are inseparable.

## 🔗 Connections
- Serves under [[Angron]] as part of the World Eaters’ inner war-band structure.
- Clashes directly with [[Lotara Sarrin]], whose sense of duty he violates.
- Operates in the same brutal command sphere as [[Khârn]].

## 📝 Notes
- Delvarus matters less as a strategist than as proof that the Legion’s pride now actively endangers its own survival.
