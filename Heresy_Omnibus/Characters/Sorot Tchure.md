## 🧠 Summary
A Word Bearers captain whose outward warmth and cultivated ties with the Ultramarines become part of the trap at [[Calth]]. Tchure embodies the personal, intimate nature of the XVII Legion’s betrayal.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Takes part in the conjunction with the Ultramarines under the pretence of alliance.
- Meets with [[Honorius Luciel]], using old familiarity to reinforce the illusion that nothing is wrong.
- Welcomes [[Kor Phaeron]] aboard the *Samothrace* once the attack is underway.
- Serves as one of the Word Bearers officers who turn trust into a battlefield weapon during the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 🔗 Connections
- Operates under [[Kor Phaeron]] and the wider design of [[Lorgar Aurelian|Lorgar]].
- His interaction with [[Honorius Luciel]] crystallises the cruelty of the Word Bearers’ deception.

## 📝 Notes
- Tchure is less important for brute force than for what he reveals about how long and carefully the betrayal has been prepared.
