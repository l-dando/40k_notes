## 🧠 Summary
A Word Bearers captain who helps enact the Legion’s betrayal at [[Calth]], embodying the cold certainty with which their faith is translated into massacre.

## ⚔️ Role in Books
- [[Know No Fear]]: Serves as one of the field commanders driving the Word Bearers’ ambush and subsequent campaign during the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 🔗 Connections
- Operates under the wider authority of [[Kor Phaeron]] and [[Erebus]].
- One of the named traitor officers central to the Calth atrocity.
