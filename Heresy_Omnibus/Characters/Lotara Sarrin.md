## 🧠 Summary
Lotara Sarrin is the mortal flag-captain of the World Eaters flagship *Conqueror*. In a Legion increasingly ruled by rage and collapse, she stands out because she still expects ships to be fought properly, orders to be obeyed, and duties to be met.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Betrayer]]
- Keeps the *Conqueror* functioning during the Shadow Crusade while much of the Legion slides toward indiscipline.
- Shares Khârn’s concern over Angron’s worsening condition and the damage the Nails are doing to the Legion.
- Holds the flagship together during the battle above Armatura, where too many World Eaters abandon shipboard duty for the surface war.
- Personally punishes [[Delvarus]] for abandoning his post and later tries to prevent the traitor fleet from being overwhelmed at Nuceria.

## 🔗 Connections
- Commands the *Conqueror* in service to [[Angron]] and the World Eaters.
- Works closely with [[Khârn]], who treats her as one of the few voices worth hearing.
- In constant conflict with the recklessness of warriors such as [[Delvarus]].

## 📝 Notes
- Lotara gives *Betrayer* one of its clearest non-Astartes perspectives on Legion madness.
- Her authority matters because it shows how much the World Eaters still depend on competence they barely deserve.
