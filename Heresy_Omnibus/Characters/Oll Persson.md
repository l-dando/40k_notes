## 🧠 Summary
A civilian living on [[Calth]] whose perspective grounds the novel away from legion command. Through Oll Persson, the destruction of the muster is felt as a human catastrophe as much as an Astartes war.

## ⚔️ Role in [[Know No Fear]]
- Lives an apparently ordinary life on Calth before sensing that something vast and wrong is building.
- Survives the devastation unleashed when wreckage and tidal destruction hit the planet.
- Moves through the ruins with other survivors, showing what the betrayal means below the level of primarchs and captains.

## 🔗 Connections
- Tied directly to [[Calth]] as the novel’s strongest civilian viewpoint.
- His survival thread complements the legionary scale of the [[Battle of Calth]].

## 📝 Notes
- Persson widens the novel’s scope, reminding the reader that the Heresy destroys populations as well as armies.
