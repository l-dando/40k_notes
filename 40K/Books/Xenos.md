# 🧭 Overview

---

Inquisitor [[Gregor Eisenhorn]] of the Ordo Xenos begins a wide‑ranging investigation into forbidden lore, hidden cult networks, and the lingering threat posed by [[Pontius Glaw]]. What starts as a hunt for heresy and xenos contamination spreads from violent frontier operations to the salons and estates of [[Gudrun]], drawing new allies such as [[Alizebeth Bequin]] and the young psyker [[Gideon Ravenor]] into Eisenhorn’s orbit.

As the inquiry deepens, Eisenhorn is forced to confront the allure of using tainted knowledge against even darker enemies. The search for the [[Necroteuch]] and the truth behind Glaw’s survival makes *Xenos* the foundation of Eisenhorn’s long moral slide, while the [[Pontius Glaw Investigation]] becomes a story of investigation, compromise, and the cost of fighting corruption from within the Imperium’s own shadows.
